Support

Answers about saving and restoring your diary.

Questions and feedback: heleg.chist@gmail.com.

The app itself is currently in Russian. Menu names below are given in English with the Russian label in brackets.

How do I save my diary?

In the app open Settings («Настройки») → Data («Данные») → Export to file («Экспорт в файл») and choose where to save the JSON file. On the same screen you can turn on automatic backups to a folder on the device or connect Google Drive.

How do I restore my data?

Open Settings → Data → Import from file («Импорт из файла») and choose how to apply the copy: Replace everything («Заменить всё») leaves exactly what is in the file, Add («Добавить») adds what is missing and deletes nothing. Then pick the saved copy of your diary. If the copy is in Google Drive, pick it in the system file picker when Drive is available on the device, or save the file to the device first.

How do I disconnect Google Drive?

Open Settings → Data → Google Drive («Google Диск») and turn backups off. If you wish, also revoke Pechora's access in your Google Account settings. Copies created earlier stay in your Drive until you delete them.
